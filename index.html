Asistent

A private, personal assistant used by its owner only.

Asistent is a self-hosted application that helps one person keep track of bills, due dates and other obligations. It reads the owner's own Gmail messages, calendar and contacts (read-only), finds invoices and payment deadlines, and adds reminders to a dedicated calendar named “Asistent” in the owner's own Google account.

It is not a public service. It cannot be signed up for, and access is limited to the owner's own Google accounts. The application never sends email, never changes or deletes mail, and never makes payments.
